AI · Research · Policy

Policy Scanner

Automated monitoring and structuring of policy and regulatory developments relevant to digital asset teams.

The experiment is about reducing the manual work involved in finding, filtering and organizing changes across many public sources while keeping human review at the end of the workflow.

AI · Intelligence · CRM

Business Intelligence Scanner

Tracks organizations across public signals to surface relevant developments, partnerships and activity.

Designed around a practical operating need: keep relationship and member intelligence current without requiring a team to manually monitor every company, website and news source.

AI · Vision · Automation

Image Intelligence Scanner

Experiments with automated analysis, classification and organization of visual information.

The focus is less on a standalone image product and more on how visual inputs can become structured signals inside larger research and operating workflows.

Ethereum · Institutions · Infrastructure

Ethereum for Digital Assets

Research and tooling that maps Ethereum infrastructure and protocol capabilities to institutional digital asset requirements.

The questions are practical: what is ready, what constraints matter, what standards are relevant, which teams need to be involved, and how an institutional requirement translates into a technical architecture.

Agents · Payments · Ethereum · APIs

Agentic Payment Bridge

A prototype exploring how agents can connect Ethereum payment rails, Google services and emerging payment standards.

The project looks at the boundary between agentic software, programmable payments and the standards needed for those systems to interact with existing infrastructure.

AI · Agents · Operations

Agentic Operations

AI-assisted workflows connecting research, CRM, outreach, events, standards management and communications.

The operating model is intentionally lean: agents handle repeatable collection, structuring and coordination tasks; people remain responsible for judgment, relationships and decisions.

Tokenization · Identity · Standards

Tokenization & Identity Research

Research into tokenized assets, settlement, digital identity, KYC, credentials and the control frameworks institutions need around digital asset infrastructure.

The aim is to connect market structure and business requirements to the technical standards and implementation choices underneath them.

Protocol · Research · Institutions

Institutional Ethereum Intelligence

Translates Ethereum protocol development and network changes into practical implications for institutional teams.

This work connects protocol practitioners with institutions around what is changing, when it matters, what the operational impact may be and where institutions can ask questions or engage earlier.
