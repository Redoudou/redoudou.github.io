# Redwan Meslem


I help global institutions adopt Ethereum and decentralized systems through digital asset strategy, tokenization and open standards.

I have served as **Executive Director of the Enterprise Ethereum Alliance** since January 2025. My work connects institutional requirements with Ethereum teams through research, partnerships, standards and ecosystem programs.

Before EEA, I led developer tooling and blockchain infrastructure programs at **ChainSafe Systems**, including work on web3.js. My earlier career spans aerospace, investment banking and enterprise technology.

I have participated in an ERC-3643 Association delegation to the **SEC Crypto Task Force**, and spoken at **Sibos, SmartCon and EthCC**.
